About the show

Art, authorship & the machinery of authenticity

SLOP is a pop-forward comedy about authorship, labor, friendship, commercialization, and what it means to make something with machines.

It is knowingly co-written with the technologies it puts onstage — the language models are in the cast list, and they get lines. Mia must decide not whether technology is good or bad, but whether her hand — and her choice — remain present in anything that bears her name.
