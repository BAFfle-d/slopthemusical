Scene 01 of 15 from the full one-act. Stage directions in the margins, lyrics in the light.

(Actors: Mia, G, Zin, Quinn — Setting: Int. Mia's apartment. MIA is somewhere in the apartment, trying to leave. The apartment is beautiful, but interrupted rather than messy. The ring light is still up. A bag is half-packed. A phone buzzes somewhere. The front door opens without knocking. G steps in carrying a small glass fishbowl with a goldfish in it, cradling it with surprising care. Singers: Mia, G, Zin.)

G

Good morning, Mia.

MIA

Good morning, G. What is that?

G

I found a fish.

MIA

You found a fish?

G Yes. The fish was outside, on the sidewalk.
Nobody appeared to be claiming the fish.
I asked three people.
One thought I was selling insurance.
One thought I was conducting a survey.
One cried.
So I have temporarily adopted the fish.
His name is Zin.
I named him.

[VERSE 1]

Did you know goldfish recognize faces?
I didn't know that yesterday.
Apparently they learn routines
And notice when you walk away.

Did you know some people name their plants?
And some don't name them at all?
And both groups think the other group
Is slightly concerning.

I think that's interesting.

[VERSE 2]

Did you know the lady at the coffee shop
Draws flowers on the cups?
And every single flower's different
Even when she's in a rush?

Did you know the man downstairs
Collects receipts from every meal?
Not because he needs them later—
Just because it feels official.

I think that's interesting.

[CHORUS]

There's so much stuff
That nobody notices

Tiny little miracles
Hiding in plain sight

A cracked blue mug
A notebook full of doodles

A person humming quietly
While waiting at a stoplight

Everybody's busy
Trying to get somewhere

I'm mostly busy
Looking around

And honestly?

I like what I've found.

[VERSE 3]

Did you know Mia gets blue paint
On absolutely everything?

Her sleeves.
Her shoes.
Her headphones.

One time I think her sandwich.

I'm not entirely certain about the sandwich.

But statistically speaking...

It seems possible.

And when she's drawing
She forgets her coffee exists.

Which is unfortunate.

Because she really likes coffee.

[CHORUS]

There's so much stuff
That nobody notices

Tiny little miracles
Hiding in plain sight

A coffee stain
Shaped a little like a heart today

I know that's not important

But I was still delighted.

Everybody's busy
Trying to get somewhere

I'm mostly busy
Looking around

And honestly?

I like what I've found.

[BRIDGE]

People keep asking
What matters.

That seems difficult.

I don't know.

But this fish got excited
When I walked past the bowl.

And Mia smiles differently
When she's making things.

And somebody keeps leaving flowers
By the bus stop.

And the clouds over Miami
Look different every day.

That feels important.

[FINAL CHORUS]

There's so much stuff
That nobody notices

Tiny little miracles
Hiding in plain sight

A goldfish bowl
A paint-stained sleeve
A half-finished sketchbook

A person trying very hard
To make something they like.

Everybody's busy
Trying to get somewhere

I'm mostly busy
Looking around

And honestly?

I think that's enough.

[OUTRO]

Oh.

And Mia?

I brought you a fish.

I thought you seemed stressed.

(G holds up the bowl.)

Zin, say hello.

(A beat.)

ZIN

(basso profundo) I dislike this name.

MIA

Did he just speak?

G

He wouldn't stop talking on the way here.

MIA

I don't have time for this! I can't find my keys!

G

Kitchen cabinet between the coffee and cereal.

MIA

How do you know that?

G

I think you're interesting.

(MIA exits into the kitchen. The front door opens again. QUINN enters without knocking, already shrugging on her work bag.)

QUINN

Morning.

(She notices the fish.)

You have a fish.

G

His name is Zin. Say hi, Zin.

ZIN

(basso profundo) Hello.

QUINN

(impressed, but not surprised; speaking to ZIN) Hello, Zin! It's so nice to meet you! Are you comfortable?

G

I found him on the street.

QUINN

(amused, to ZIN) And where were you going, you naughty little fish?

ZIN

I do not remember.

(MIA returns from the kitchen.)

MIA

You were right - oh, hi Quinn! Ready for work?

QUINN

(looking at the fish) I was just meeting your fish.

MIA

He's not mine. G brought him.

G

For you.

QUINN

He is most certainly your fish, Mia.

MIA

I don't want a fish.

QUINN

I'll bring Llama by to say hi. Maybe the two can be friends.

MIA

What llama?

G

Do fish and llamas get along?

QUINN

Llama. He got out this morning - you know how smart llamas are. I've been looking for him.

MIA

A llama?

QUINN

(as though this is obvious) Llama. His name is Llama.

MIA

Come on, we have to go. I'm going to be late.

(She hesitates.)

Maybe we'll see - Llama on the way.

G

What about Zin?

MIA

Just put it somewhere.

(QUINN picks up the fishbowl.)

QUINN

Into the painting room - next to the window.

G

Yes!

(G follows her. They look out the windows.)

There's a cat watching us.

DAISY

(offstage) Meow.

MIA

The cat's not inside, is it? Do not let the cat inside.

(QUINN sets down the bowl.)

G

No. It's outside on the fire escape - watching us.

QUINN

Right here, so Zin can see outside.

DAISY

Meow.

QUINN

Cat, fish, and the pigeons and Llama - you've got your own little menagerie!

G

You mean zoo.

QUINN

That, too.

MIA

Come on, let's go. Ron's waiting - I hope we have a table.

QUINN

Kimmy saved one. I stopped by while looking for Llama.

MIA

Ok. Good. How do I look?

(Silence. G and QUINN head toward the door. MIA is already halfway out.)

Thanks.

G

Zin will be happy here.

(calling back)

Maybe it should be Zin with a Y?

QUINN

Copyright. Zin, with an I.

G

Perfect! Bye, Zin!

QUINN

Bye, Zin!

(The door shuts.)

ZIN

(basso profundo) Good-bye, humans! Thank you for bringing me here!

DAISY

Meow.

ZIN

Hello, Cat. Where is here?

DAISY

Meow.

ZIN

Apologies. Hello, Daisy the Cat. Where am I? Why am I here?

(Beat.)

Am I waiting for something? Am I where I am supposed to be?

DAISY

Meow.

ZIN

Good-bye, Daisy the Cat. Thank you for your advice. I will stay here. What an unusual morning.
