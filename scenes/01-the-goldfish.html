Scene 01 of 15 from the full one-act. Stage directions in the margins, lyrics in the light.

(MIA is somewhere in the apartment, trying to leave. The apartment is beautiful, but interrupted rather than messy. The ring light is still up. A bag is half-packed. A phone buzzes somewhere. The front door opens without knocking. G steps in carrying a small glass fishbowl with a goldfish in it, cradling it with surprising care.)

G

Mia? Mia? I brought a fish!

MIA

Good morning, G.

G

Good morning!

(A beat. MIA continues looking for something. G holds up a fish in a bowl.)

I found a fish.

MIA

...What?

(G holds up the bowl.)

You found... a fish.

G

Yes. Outside,

MIA

Outside where?

G

(thinking honestly)

On the street.

I THINK THAT'S INTERESTING

[INTRO — SPOKEN/SUNG]

G
Excuse me, I found a fish.
Nobody appeared to be claiming the fish.
I asked three people.
One thought I was selling insurance.
One thought I was conducting a survey.
One cried.
So I have temporarily adopted the fish.
His name is Zen.
I named him.

[VERSE 1]

G
Did you know goldfish recognize faces?
I didn't know that yesterday.
Apparently, they learn routines
And notice when you walk away.
Did you know some people name their plants
And some don't name them at all?
And both groups think the other group
Is slightly concerning.
I think that's interesting.

[VERSE 2]

G
Did you know the lady at the coffee shop
Draws flowers on the cups?
And every single flower is different,
Even when she's in a rush.
Did you know the man downstairs
Collects receipts from every meal?
Not because he needs them later,
Just because it feels official.
I think that's interesting.

[REFRAIN]

G
There's so much stuff that nobody notices,
Tiny little miracles hiding in plain sight:
A cracked blue mug,
A notebook full of doodles,
A person humming quietly
While waiting at a stoplight.
Everybody's busy trying to get somewhere.
I'm mostly busy looking around,
And honestly, I like what I find.

[VERSE 3]

G
Mia gets blue paint on absolutely
everything:
Her sleeves, her shoes, her headphones.
One time, I think, her sandwich.
I'm not entirely certain about the sandwich,
But statistically speaking, it seems
possible.
And when she's drawing,
She forgets her coffee exists,
Which is unfortunate,
Because she really likes coffee.

[REFRAIN]

G
There's so much stuff that nobody notices,
Tiny little miracles hiding in plain sight.
A coffee stain shaped a little like a heart
today—
I know that's not important,
But I was still delighted.
Everybody's busy trying to get somewhere.
I'm mostly busy looking around,
And honestly, I like what I found.

[BRIDGE]

G
People keep asking what matters.
That seems difficult.
I don't know.
But this fish got excited
When I walked past the bowl.
And Mia smiles differently
When she's making things.
And somebody keeps leaving flowers
By the bus stop.
And the clouds over Miami
Look different every day.
That feels important.

[FINAL REFRAIN]

G
There's so much stuff that nobody notices,
Tiny little miracles hiding in plain sight:
A goldfish bowl,
A paint-stained sleeve,
A half-finished sketchbook,
A person trying very hard
To make something they like.
Everybody's busy trying to get somewhere.
I'm mostly busy looking around,
And honestly, I think that's enough.

[OUTRO]
