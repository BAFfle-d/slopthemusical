Scene 03 of 15 from the full one-act. Stage directions in the margins, lyrics in the light.

(Characters: Kimmy, Ron, G, Mia — Setting: Int. Coffeeshop. KIMMY drops the drinks and gives MIA a long, world-weary look.)

KIMMY

Let me know what you want. We have a special on chai tea with a brand new recipe from an AI chatbot! Reception has been - not good.

RON

(a big, warm smile for MIA)

Mia. Finally. So nice to meet you, and … your friend

G

I'm G. I'm here to-

RON

(a slow turn toward G)

I'm sorry - what did you say your name was? T?

G

No, my name is G, it's short for-

RON

Dave. Got it. Now, Dave, I have very important business with Mia. We're building a future - a future with Mia as a world class artist!.

MIA

(quietly, almost to herself at first)

G, it's ok. We came to listen so let's… listen. OK?

G

Sorry, Mia. You're right. I'm here to support you.

(MIA mouths, "Thank you." RON clocks the shift immediately.)

RON

You already get it, Mia. That's great. You're sitting on a gold mine but mining it with a teaspoon. One painting at a time is noble—classic, almost Renaissance—but a new age demands a new way. It's about grabbing people's -

(A beat. He anticipates the answer.)

RON

—attention! Attention is the new currency!

RON

(With dramatic gesticulation.)

We're going to build you - an agent!

MIA

You said that on the phone. I still don't understand.

G

Mia, he's talking about -

MIA

G, I asked Ron.

(G looks shocked.)

RON

AI - artificial intelligence! It's a special computer program trained on your unique Mia look!

G

Right, but what's it gonna do?

RON

It learns the saturated blues, the hard white light, the way you paint your sunsets. Then, in just a few moments, it takes all of your style and produces thousands of assets. You would have a whole product line by lunch!

MIA

So, it's like an apprentice?

RON

Um - sure, yeah, just like that.

MIA

I mean, Warhol didn't pull every silkscreen himself. Britto doesn't personally paint every thing with his name on it. They built studios. An assistant would help me - get seen by more people by - increasing my production.

Right, Ron?

RON

Exactly! You don't want less authorship. You want more reach. You stay rare. You pay the rent. You get the acclaim. You get seen by millions.

MIA

What good am I as an artist if no one sees my work?

G

Your work, not -

RON

The agent is a studio that scales. You could plod through and hope this one piece gets seen by the right person at the right time in the right place -

MIA

Or I could scale and let the whole world see it

(KIMMY skates past and drops a Danish in front of MIA.)

MIA

I didn't order this.

KIMMY

From that alt-punk guy with the blue spiky hair and the piercings. A regular. Says you're cute. Keep an open mind.

(KIMMY skates away.)

(That's where the published page ends — the scene continues with "Chef's Kiss" in the full draft.)
