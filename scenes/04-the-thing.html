(The scene picks up directly after RON's closing laugh. MIA and G are outside on the sidewalk.)

G

Are you sure you don't want me to come upstairs with you? Two heads are better than one for a decision this big, and I really think I could—

MIA

You are the best. I mean that. But I'm going up alone. I've met Karen and Chad. You haven't. And you're—

G

Ron didn't like me.

MIA

I didn't say that.

G

You liked what he said, though.

MIA

I'm saying it's a different world I have to walk into, and I need to walk into it as myself. Not half of a two-person act.

G

I could still help.

MIA

You are helping. Go make sure Zin's set up right. He's had a big morning, too.

G

(already turning, already halfway gone)

Oh my God. The filter. I completely—okay. Pet store. Then I'm coming straight back to check on you. Right after the pet store. Maybe also lunch. Mostly the pet store.

(G hurries off. MIA watches him go. She does not move for a moment. Then she turns toward the building but does not go in.
