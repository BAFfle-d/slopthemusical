Scene 05 of 15 from the full one-act. Stage directions in the margins, lyrics in the light.

(The sleek, polished lobby of the Engine Room. MIA enters through the front doors, takes in the room, then approaches the reception desk. QUINN sits behind it, posture perfect and hair neat, typing with clipped efficiency. She looks entirely different from the bohemian neighbor MIA knows.)

QUINN

(without looking up)

Good morning. Name and time of appointment.

MIA

(amused)

It's me.

(QUINN looks up. The professional mask instantly cracks into a massive, genuine smile.)

QUINN

Mia! Oh my God. You survived.

(She glances toward the inner glass doors. Her smile falters. She leans closer, lowering her voice.)

QUINN

We'll talk later. But they are - very consumed by you right now.

MIA

(a little unnerved)

Well, that's a bit much, but okay. They're just - excited. I am too.

QUINN

(nodding)

Good attitude - keep it up.

(The inner glass doors slide open. KAREN and CHAD emerge, slick, smiling, and radiating expensive energy. QUINN snaps back to her monitor and sits perfectly straight, though she is still vibrating with concern for MIA. KAREN holds up her phone on a small tripod. Its ring light glares.)

KAREN

There she is! Chat, I want to introduce Mia. She is an amazing creative professional that—

(KAREN pulls MIA closer, then immediately shifts her just outside the frame while adjusting the ring light.)

KAREN

Hold on. Let me get the lighting.

(Adjusts again, displeased, and again. Starts again tepidly.)

Chat, I am so excited to announce - I am so - I am so thrilled -

CHAD

(sliding into frame)

Drop a dolphin and a paintbrush in the comments if you caught Mia's canvas outside the VIP section at the Dolphins game!

KAREN

And if you weren't VIP, she was still everywhere at the game - like in the advertisements at every entrance for South Beach's busiest cosmetic surgeon, Dr. Dade.

(CHAD points to the side)

CHAD

Or for our guys — the urinal ads. Eye level. Bone-fidence: the latest breakthrough in erectile dysfunction medication. I swear by it!

KAREN

(enthusiastically)

We use it every night. Works just like a dream! Check out the link in our bio!

CHAD

But all those beautiful Miami visuals were by the Engine Room's newest star — Mia!

(They smile into the phone and nod together. The silence lasts one beat too long.)

MIA

(to QUINN)

What are they pointing to?

QUINN

(Sigh - taking notes)

Ad graphics I'll have to add later before it posts. Let me get them on track.

(She clears her throat and projects into the room.)

QUINN

You're all set up in the conference room - and that Jamaican coffee blend you like is brewing.

(KAREN stops. Her influencer smile drops for a fraction of a second, then returns.)

KAREN

Good thing we weren't live, and - wait - the Blue Mountain blend?

QUINN

Of course.

KAREN

(through her teeth)

Thank you, Quinn. We'll take it from here.

CHAD

Mia! Mia! Mia! The clients were thrilled with your artwork as part of their campaign. Their engagement's through the roof.

KAREN

They are - the color, the vibrant South Florida vibe - we've had three more clients call this morning asking how they can get the same Mia treatment.

(KAREN and CHAD begin leading MIA toward the conference room.)

CHAD

Ron spoke to you already, I know, and I want to make sure we pin down some numbers and goals -

KAREN

Chad - honey, don't bore her with numbers. The talent is there, the numbers will follow. Right, Mia?

MIA

Um - I guess? I do have a lot of questions, and a lot of it is logistical and maybe technological?

QUINN

I called IT already.

KAREN

(annoyed)

I was just about to say that. Thank you, Quinn, for your proactivity. It might be days before -

QUINN

They said they will be here soon.

KAREN

Well, I'm glad they are prioritizing our - priorities properly.

CHAD

Right - they probably need a lot of time to integrate and ingest your work and build - and do whatever it is they have to do.

MIA

(tentatively)

Ingest?

(QUINN gives MIA a reassuring nod and an okay sign.)

KAREN

Shop talk, Mia. They're not going to eat you, dear. You are the it-girl, girl.

CHAD

Yeah—the ate-it girl. Chopped, cooked, and ate!

(KAREN smiles through her annoyance.)

MIA

Aren't those bad things?

KAREN

Not if I say they aren't. Sweetie, the Engine Room is not some fly-by-night digital marketing agency. Our narrative is bulletproof.

CHAD

And the metrics prove it. We drive traffic. We get the numbers that get you paid.

KAREN

And all you have to do is be you and be amazing.

CHAD

Exactly! Amazing!

(CHAD is about to speak but is interrupted by a loud, aggressive eight-bit buzzer from the stairwell. His smile freezes into a grimace.)

QUINN

The twins are here.

KAREN

They work in the basement - they call it the engine room.

MIA

Isn't the agency the Engine Room?

CHAD

(rubbing his temples)

The basement is the Engine Room's engine room. They claimed it first.

KAREN

(earnestly)

Can't argue with that logic.

(Speaking straight to MIA)

They are very particular. Just be yourself. Don't get frazzled.

CHAD

They are the lifeline of this agency. Just be cool.

MIA

And they're twins?

(The stairwell door swings open. JIM and BART emerge wearing hoodies and sunglasses. They move with a strange, synchronized gait. They do not smile. They nod at QUINN. QUINN smiles and nods back. KAREN and CHAD step aside, allowing the twins to take center stage.)

BART

(from behind)

Yes. Jim and I are here for ingestion of the talent.

JIM

Is she ready?

MIA

(startled)

Yes, I am. I'm ready for anything.

BART

Good. Come with us.

JIM

We will take -

JIM and BART

The freight elevator.

(JIM and BART turn and walk away. KAREN and CHAD propel MIA after them. MIA looks back at QUINN. QUINN mouths, ‘It's okay.’ MIA follows the twins out. QUINN collects two large folders and joins KAREN and CHAD.)

KAREN

I can't imagine how Quinn got them here so quickly. They never even answer my calls.

CHAD

She says they're nice to her. I mean, they're all a little—

(QUINN walks towards them; KAREN cuts him off)

KAREN

QUINN! Are those our overnights?

(QUINN sets the folders down. KAREN claims one; CHAD takes the other and peers eagerly over her shoulder.)

Gimme gimme - I wanna see! Oh - Look! They called me iconic!

CHAD

And so many comments, and likes, and shares!

QUINN

Oh my!

(KAREN and CHAD roll their eyes.)

QUINN — INTRO

Good morning! The sentiment's kind.
Engagement's up and the brand's aligned.
Have y'all heard the one where the bots all meet?
They just keep talking, got nobody to greet.
Dead Internet Theory—I read the whole thread.
Half of it's nonsense, half lives in my head.
If bots are all talking while we're all asleep,
I wonder what promises they get to keep.

CHAD — VERSE

See, here's the thing about scale, little Quinn:
You don't need a soul if the numbers come in.
We A/B tested authenticity back in Q3.
Turns out vulnerability plays best at 10:23.
So we prompt the persona, we fine-tune the feel.
You've got a voice? Great. Let's make it a wheel.
Spin it, clone it, render, repeat.
Infinite music at a fraction of heat.
ROI on a human is messy and slow.
ROI on a model? You already know.
We feed it the portfolio, train on the style.
Output ten thousand assets. File, file, file.

KAREN — VERSE

I filmed my morning routine at two p.m.
The sun's a LUT, the gratitude's a GM.
My therapist says I'm performing my life.
I said, ‘That's the brand, babe. That's the wife.’
Five years of content, and what's really me?
The ring light, the presets, the sponsored green tea.
But the comments say ‘QUEEN’ and the DMs say ‘GOALS,’
So who needs a self when you've got analytics and souls?
I cried real tears once in a sponsored post.
Engagement went down, so I never got close
To that again. Now I plan all my grief
Three business days out for maximum reach.

QUINN — PRE-CHORUS

I'm still learning how all of this works.
Everybody here has their own little quirks.
If the whole world's changing, I want to see
Who we're becoming and who we might be.
Honestly... That's enough for me.

ALL — CHORUS

Nothing's real online, and that's okay!
Curate the chaos, monetize the gray.
Hallucinate the meaning, scale the empty space.
Nothing's real online—

CHAD

—and I love this place!

KAREN — VERSE

I was an artist once. I think. Maybe.
Before the metrics became my baby.
Before I learned what the grid rewards:
Consistency. Frequency. Emotional chords.

CHAD

Now I teach it. Now I sell it. The method, the magic,
the way to tell it. So the algorithm hears your cry
And lifts you up before you die—
—in obscurity. Obviously. In obscurity.

CHAD — BRIDGE

If the internet's dead, then we're dancing on graves.
If the users are bots, then the bots are our slaves.
If nothing is real, then there's nothing to lose.
If nothing is real, then the winner's the one who can—

KAREN AND CHAD — BRIDGE

—curate the best version of nothing at all!
Filter the void till the void looks like a mall!
If nobody's watching, then everyone's free
To be anything, anyone, endlessly—

KAREN — PRE-CHORUS

I used to want to make something that stayed.
Now I just want to make something that pays.
And pays. And pays. And pays. Honestly...
Same difference most days.

ALL — FINAL CHORUS

Nothing's real online, and that's okay!
Curate the chaos, monetize the gray.
Hallucinate the meaning, scale the empty space.
Nothing's real online—

QUINN

—and I kind of love this place.

ALL — OUTRO

Nothing's real online, and that's okay with me.
The dead internet's quiet, and the quiet's free.
No one to disappoint. No one to fail.
Just the hum of the server and the next scheduled —

CHAD

—email. Obviously. The next scheduled email.

QUINN

Would you like me to prompt a more authentic version of this meeting?

(Blackout.)
