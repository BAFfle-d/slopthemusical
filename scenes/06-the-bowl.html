The Bowl.

Scene 06 of 15 from the full one-act. Stage directions in the margins, lyrics in the light.

(MIA's apartment. Late afternoon. G enters holding a small bag from a pet store.)

G

Zin? Zin! I'm home! I brought you—

(He stops. ZIN floats upside down in his dirty bowl, motionless.)

G

Oh no! Oh no, no, no, no—

(He rushes to the bowl, picks it up, and stares at ZIN's lifeless form.)

G

What have I done? I came too late! It's all my fault!

(He looks toward the bathroom. A terrible idea forms.)

G

I have to flush him. And get Mia another fish. She can't know. She's been so stressed. This would break her. I have to be strong. For Mia.

(He starts toward the bathroom, cradling the bowl like a coffin.)

ZIN

(suddenly awake; laughing)

Whoa, whoa, whoa! I know you!

(G freezes. ZIN swims upright, still laughing.)

ZIN

I was playing a game! The pigeons told me it would be funny! Did you see? I was upside down! For like—I don't know—a while?

(He stops laughing and considers.)

ZIN

That was funny. I think. I was laughing, so it must have been.

G

(still breathing hard)

It wasn't funny. I thought you were dead.

ZIN

(matter-of-fact)

The water is very dirty. I'm pooping where I sleep. I assumed I was dead, too.

G

(setting the bowl down carefully)

You're at home. You live here with Mia. She's at work. I'm G.

ZIN

A, B, C, D, E, F, G—I am being carried by a letter of the alphabet.

(ZIN notices the bag.)

ZIN

What's in the bag?

G

Oh! I brought you—

(He pulls out a small container of fish flakes.)

G

—food. And—

(He pulls out a plastic plant and castle.)

G

—a castle! And a plant!

ZIN

(staring at them)

...It's plastic.

G

(crestfallen)

You don't like it? I can go back and get something else.

ZIN

They are for me?

(G nods.)

ZIN

Then I love them. I would like to take a tour of my castle, please.

(G brightens. He drops the castle into the bowl. ZIN makes approving sounds. G adds the plant, which is larger than the castle.)

ZIN

I have a tree.

G

It's a plant.

ZIN

(looking up)

It provides shade. It's a tree.

(ZIN considers the label.)

ZIN

Made in— What's a China?

G

It's a country.

ZIN

Can we go there? It sounds like a lovely place.
