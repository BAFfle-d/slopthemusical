Scene 08 of 15 from the full one-act. Stage directions in the margins, lyrics in the light.

(Characters: Mme. Le Chat, Daisy, Mia — Setting: Int. the Engine Room lobby and then Le Chat's office. The agency reception area. QUINN sits behind the desk, typing. Her hair is neatly pulled back. MIA enters from the Engine Room.)

MIA

(awkward)

So... how is your day going?

QUINN

(without looking up, matter-of-fact)

Fine. Busy. The usual. How was downstairs?

MIA

(excited)

Oh my God, Quinn - Jim and Bart said they can build me an empire! I{D}m going to be everywhere!

QUINN

(distracted)

Mm. Sounds nice.

(A long beat.)

MIA

(deflated)

Yeah. It was… nice. Thank you for - everything.

QUINN

(small smile, does not look up, bored)

Of course. My pleasure. Karen is a little delayed for your meeting.

MIA

Oh, that's OK, I'll wait. Thank you.

(A silence.)

I like your hair up, by the way. You should wear it like that more often. It opens up your face.

(A beat.)

QUINN

That's very kind of you, Mia. Thank you.

(DAISY hops onto QUINN's desk.)

DAISY

Meow.

QUINN

It appears your schedule has changed.

MIA

No more vlogging with Karen?

QUINN

Madame Le Chat will see you first.

MIA

Who?

QUINN

She built the Engine Room - and she's very picky. Follow Daisy.

(The lights shift. DAISY leads MIA into MADAME LE CHAT's domain. LE CHAT is already standing, watching MIA enter. A long silence.)

LE CHAT

Mia. A pleasure.

MIA

(sitting)

The pleasure is mine.

LE CHAT

Are you always so polite? And so - clean.

MIA

Well... I'm sorry. I didn't notice the paint on my sleeves this morning.

LE CHAT

Your sleeves tell me that you are a painter, or were.

(A beat.)

I like your aesthetic. What are your comps?

MIA

My comps? I don't have any - I haven't really looked.

LE CHAT

You didn't take your work anywhere else? Other galleries? Managers?

MIA

Quinn helped me. Art is all about networking, right?

LE CHAT

Not right - not wrong, either.

(Pause)

Quinn has a good eye.

MIA

She's been a good friend. Everyone here has been so -

LE CHAT

(interrupting)

Stop.

(Silence)

I expect children to feed me pleasantries and platitudes about how nice people are.

Everyone in this building is exceptional. Ron is. Karen and Chad - (beat) - are. Me, you. Exceptional.

MIA

(softly)

Thank you. That's a huge compliment.

LE CHAT

Don't get comfortable. Don't think that this is your big discovery or something. Dream bigger than here. You cannot stop growing. Don't let your work become -

MIA

Slop?

LE CHAT

Slop, yes. Slop will pay your rent. And the agency's, and everyone here. But you are an artist. Know the difference between your artistic vision and - everything else.

MIA

What if everything else pays the bills, though? Isn't there something to that?

LE CHAT

I wish it was as easy as Basquiat stumbling into Warhol.

Your talent is obvious, and your education is apparent in everything you paint. You'll have lots of opportunities to make money.

MIA

Ron says that I could be on - I mean, my art could be on-

LE CHAT

Mass-produced junk in Walmart? Saccharine greeting cards from Hallmark?

You are an artist first, but, yes, slop will pay your rent.

MIA

I could be an artist that pays the bills, can't I? I'll produce more here. More people will see my work.

LE CHAT

On erectile dysfunction ads.

You set out to create beauty. Will you settle for boner pills, instead?

MIA

No! I - that's - I hadn't - no, that's not what I want, either.

LE CHAT

Good. I have walked many young people into their first big break, and -

(Sighs)

Well, success is an excellent distraction from your dreams,

THE PAINTBRUSH AND THE PAYCHECK — MME. LE CHAT

I have seen the girls who wash their hands of color.
I have watched them trade their shadows for a desk.
They call it ‘pivot,’ ‘scale,’ ‘another dollar’—
I call it beautiful, befouled, and picturesque.
A student once asked Picasso, ‘What do I see?’
He said, ‘Whatever isn't looking back at me.’
I had clean nails and a brand-new degree,
But my canvas sat there empty as a receipt.
You walked in with smudges on your sleeve,
The kind that take three nights to achieve.
Not the mess of a hobby, not the stain of a trend—
The wreckage of someone who stayed until the end.
I am the claw, I am the purr,
The velvet-gloved provocateur.
You can keep your paycheck, keep your brief;
I'll take my bruise and call it relief.
I am the cat on the warm windowsill,
The one who watches when the lights go still.
Not for the likes, not for the retweet—
For my paint still drying at my feet.
They say the goldfish forgets in three seconds flat.
I say the goldfish remembers what the human forgets: the mat
Where you kneeled, the brush you bit, the curse
You swallowed whole and turned into a verse.
Chad wants metrics, Karen wants a deck.
I want the girl they haven't flattened yet,
Trying to make one ugly, true, small thing
That no algorithm will ever want to sing.
Let me tell you a secret, ma chérie:
The mouse does not ask the cat for mercy.
And the cat does not ask the clock for time.
The paint does not ask the canvas for a sign.
You already know where the trouble starts:
When you stop feeling the hunger and start playing the parts.
I am the claw, I am the purr,
The shadow that the suits prefer to blur.
You can take your bonus, take your flight—
I'll take my empty studio at night.
I am the cat on the cold fire escape,
The one who knows there's nothing left to fake.
Not for the pitch, not for applause—
For my fingerprints beneath the gloss.
So where are you going, little mammal with the stained wrist?
Back to the bowl? Back to the list?
No.
You're coming with me.
And we're going to make a mess they'll never see.

LE CHAT

Now show me your artist space. I want to see your creative space, and -

DAISY

Meow.

LE CHAT

A llama? In the lobby? How?

(Her phone rings. LE CHAT answers.)

Yes, I am aware of the llama. Is this a crisis that I need to attend?

(beat.)

I'll be down. Don't feed it.

MIA

Is it Quinn's llama?

LE CHAT

It appears it must be. Spiky blue hair and everything.

Come along. None of the other owners will show up - they're far too smart for that.

MIA

You own the building?

LE CHAT

Of course. I got bored. Husband number 5 - God rest his soul - left me very comfortable.

When you have everything you need, what do you do with your time?

I bought a building. What would you do?

MIA

(Starts to answer)

LE CHAT

Think longer about your answer. First, the llama.

(They follow DAISY toward the lobby. Lights shift.)
