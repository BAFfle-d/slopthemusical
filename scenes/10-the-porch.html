Scene 10 of 15 from the full one-act. Stage directions in the margins, lyrics in the light.

(Outside MIA and QUINN's building. Continuous from the lobby. G finishes placing the two chairs and helps MIA and QUINN sit. The lights settle into the porch. A quiet beat.)

MIA

That was quite - something. Are you - okay?

QUINN

I was sharp with you today.

(A beat.)

MIA

Are you apologizing?

QUINN

Diagnosing. I saw you getting excited about the paycheck -

MIA

And you were afraid I'd forget I was a painter first.

(Quinn nods.)

Apology accepted.

(MIA and QUINN hug. G runs up and joins the group hug.)

QUINN & MIA

G!

G

What? I felt that.

MIA

Where were you keeping a llama anyways?

QUINN

Oh, you know, he comes and goes.

G

You just let him - wander about?

QUINN

This is new. He came with a side project of mine.

MIA

What side project?

QUINN

Llama food. Online retail. I've cornered the market.

(MIA laughs.)

MIA

Llama food? You've got to be kidding? Right, right?

G

Not much competition, I imagine.

MIA

Quinn - you're not serious?

QUINN

It pays the bills. And a bit more.

(Clears throat.)

I clear four - five figures a week, depending.

(MIA goes quiet.)

G

That is a lot of llama food.

QUINN

Not a lot of competition. I am the queen of llama food.

(Beat.)

It's all automated. I set it up and - boom - money. And, then, Llama.

MIA

So the job at the agency -?

QUINN

I liked the energy. I learned a lot. And, of course, the universe needed me to bring you.

(Matter-of-factly.)

And I got bored. Everything on autopilot - I was just sitting. So, why not?

(KIMMY comes around the building on skates, a little out of breath but triumphant.)

KIMMY

He refused to be tied. Llama's in the back, grazing.

QUINN

Thank you, Kimmy.

KIMMY

Somebody had to - just watch out - he's in a spitting mood.

QUINN

He didn't get you, did he?

KIMMY

No. Got Ron right on the blazer - thought it was a pigeon.

G

Pigeons - man - they are everywhere.

(KIMMY looks from MIA to QUINN.)

KIMMY

I have to head back to the coffee shop - post llama cleanup. Come on, G. Milkshake? Danish? It's on me.

G

(waves at MIA and QUINN) Bye! She's getting me a milkshake! A milkshake!

(KIMMY rolls away. G follows. MIA and QUINN watch them go.)

QUINN

(yells after G) And a Danish. (to MIA) I never ate the Danish you sent me the day we first met. I appreciated it, though.

MIA

I didn't send you a Danish. In fact, just this morning, she said I got a Danish from - Oh.

(A beat.)

OH!

(A beat.)

Well, good for G. May his milkshake be milky-

(They settle into the quiet.)

QUINN

(laughs) And his Danish be moist.

MIA

(laughs) Gross, Quinn.

(Puts a hand on QUINN's shoulder.)

I'm gonna go get some work done. Can I - I mean, are you gonna be up? I might need a friend with good taste to tell me if I'm crazy.

QUINN

Mia, no matter what you do - even if you sell out and become fabulously wealthy -

(A beat.)

No matter what, I'm your friend. It's your choice, and I will support you.

MIA

Thank you, Quinn.

(MIA goes inside, leaving QUINN alone on the porch as the music starts.)

QUINN

You're welcome, Mia.

QUINN — VERSE 1

I've been studying humans.
I keep little notes.
You buy tiny notebooks,
Then lose them in coats.
You say, “This is garbage,”
Then make something great.
You burn toast three mornings,
Then blame it on fate.
And you walk in with blue paint
On your sleeve and your shoe,
Like the universe sneezed
And it landed on you.

QUINN — CHORUS

And people don't fall into paintings.
That's not what they do.
They fall into fingerprints,
Tiny pieces of you.
The laugh that escapes you,
The coffee-stain heart,
The weird little habits
You leave in your art.

QUINN — VERSE 2

I've been studying humans.
You cry at odd things:
Old men in sweaters
And children on swings.
You keep all your bad drafts
In boxes and piles,
Then stare at the ceiling
For miles and for miles.
Then suddenly sit up
Like lightning arrived,
And spend three straight hours
Completely alive.

QUINN — CHORUS

Because people don't fall into paintings.
That's not what they do.
They fall into fingerprints,
Tiny pieces of you.
The smile that appears
When you finally start,
The strange little echoes
You leave in your art.

QUINN — BRIDGE

They'll tell you, “Fix edges,”
Or, “Clean up that line.”
Make this more better.
Make that more refined.
But I've looked at perfection,
And it never felt like home—
Not like the sweet little chaos
Or a warm pool of light from the sun.
(spoken) Honestly? Perfect is mostly decorative.

QUINN — BRIDGE 2

I've seen ten thousand sunsets
Go floating online—
Perfect little colors, perfect little skies.
But I remember the girl
With the paint on her face
Who forgot where she put her keys
Twice. In the same place.

QUINN — FINAL CHORUS

'Cause people don't fall into paintings.
That's never the part.
They fall into seeing
Who stood at the start.
You aren't just the brushstroke
Or one polished part.
You're all of the little things
Walking through art.

QUINN — OUTRO

I've been studying humans.
I think I've got one—
Mostly figured out.
Mostly.
And I kind of adore her.
