Scene 10 of 15 from the full one-act. Stage directions in the margins, lyrics in the light.

(Outside MIA and QUINN's building. Continuous from the lobby. G finishes placing the two chairs and helps MIA and QUINN sit. The lights settle into the porch. A quiet beat.)

MIA

That was quite a day at the office. Are you - fine?

QUINN

I was sharp with you today.

(A beat.)

MIA

Are you apologizing?

QUINN

Diagnosing. I saw you getting excited about the paycheck -

MIA

And you were afraid I'd forget I was a painter first.

(Quinn nods.)

Apology accepted.

(MIA and QUINN hug. G runs up and joins the group hug.)

QUINN & MIA

G!

G

What? I felt that.

MIA

Where were you keeping a llama anyways?

QUINN

Oh, you know, he comes and goes.

G

You just let him - wander about?

QUINN

This is new. He came with a side project of mine.

MIA

What side project?

QUINN

Llama food. Online retail. I've cornered the market.

(MIA laughs.)

MIA

Llama food? You've got to be kidding? Right, right?

G

Not much competition, I imagine.

MIA

Quinn - What aren't you telling me?

QUINN

It pays the bills, and could pay yours, and his, and - I clear five figures a week.

(MIA goes quiet.)

G

That is a lot of llama food.

QUINN

I built the store and automated most of it. It keeps going whether I'm in the room or not. Ta-da! Queen of llama food.

MIA

And the job you just got fired from?

QUINN

I get bored. I liked the energy. And, apparently, the universe had a plan for me to bring you there.

(KIMI comes around the building on skates, a little out of breath but triumphant.)

KIMMY

He refused to be tied. Llama's in the back, grazing.

QUINN

Thank you for bringing him home, Kimmy.

KIMI

Somebody had to - just watch out - he's in a spitting mood.

QUINN

He didn't get you, did he?

KIMI

No. Got Ron right on the blazer - thought it was a pigeon.

G

Pigeons - man - they are everywhere.

(KIMI looks from MIA to QUINN.)

KIMI

I have to head back to the coffee shop - post llama cleanup. Come on, G, I'll get you a milkshake - and a Danish?
