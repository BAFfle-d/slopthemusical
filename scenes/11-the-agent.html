Scene 11 of 15 from the full one-act. Stage directions in the margins, lyrics in the light.

(The next morning. MIA's apartment. ZIN circles his bowl.)

ZIN

The female person fed me three times this morning. Very generous. I feel I should be suspicious of inattentive but generous guests.

(He swims to his plant.)

ZIN

Petunia? She isn't speaking to me. Suspicious. Or she doesn't speak fish. Fish is niche. Maybe she's deaf.

(MIA enters, dressed for the agency and checking her phone. She paces, pats her pockets, looks for her keys, and mutters under her breath—the same search, the same mutter, the same as always, only faster. ZIN watches.)

ZIN

(louder)

Hello! Female person! Would you like to feed me again? I am not hungry, but I will be.

(MIA doesn't hear him. She finds her keys, then catches her reflection in the window. She sees the blue paint still marking her sleeve. Her phone buzzes. She steadies herself, pockets it, and exits.)

ZIN

Definitely deaf. Or dead. Ghost fish sign language—I don't know that language. I may be silently haunting this place.

(He considers this.)

ZIN

Though now that she's gone, I am hungry again. I can't be hungry if I'm a ghost. So: deaf.

(DAISY appears on the windowsill immediately after MIA leaves. Her attention remains fixed in the direction MIA went.)

ZIN

Daisy the Cat! Did you see her leave? Fed me three times. Very emotional.

DAISY

(without looking away)

Meow.

ZIN

Oh—the boy is gone too, isn't he? Everyone's leaving today. Maybe I'm the ghost. Trapped in a bowl. What can I do? I have no thumbs.

(DAISY meows again and moves toward the edge of the windowsill.)

ZIN

You're right. Manners. Carp my diem, Daisy. Day of the fish! Or was it "seize the fish"? I don't understand it. Maybe there's a special day where people seize fish, like it's the last one they'll ever get to seize. Good energy for them. Troublesome for me.

(DAISY starts after MIA.)

ZIN

I know cat, and llama, and pigeon, and dolphin—clicks and whistles, that one. Very niche. More than fish, even.

(DAISY is already halfway gone.)

ZIN

Already? We just started a dialogue. I felt heard even though I might be deaf.

(DAISY disappears after MIA.)

ZIN

Petunia—I'm staying right here until I figure out the female person.

(A beat.)

ZIN

(quietly)

I hope she's okay.
