Scene 13 of 15 from the full one-act. Stage directions in the margins, lyrics in the light.

(MIA's apartment. Night. A knock at the door.)

QUINN

Mia? It's me.

(MIA doesn't move.)

QUINN

Are you okay? We saw you leave and didn't say anything. Do you need anything?

MIA

I'm fine.

QUINN

Okay. I'm right downstairs if you need me.

(QUINN's footsteps recede. Another, quieter knock.)

KIMI

Courier service. Courtesy of the cat lady.

(A thick packet slides through the mail slot. KIMI knocks again. Silence. KIMI leaves. A third knock. G's voice is warm but uncertain.)

G

Mia? Kimi said she thought you were napping, and Quinn said you were fine. I believe them.

(A beat.)

G

But how's Zyn? I brought food and a new toy. He's really very delicate.

(He knocks again.)

G

I'll just leave it here. I'll come back tomorrow.

(G's footsteps recede. MIA is alone.)

(After a long pause, MIA opens the packet. She spreads the proofs across the floor and studies them one after another. She picks one up, squints at it, then holds it beside a sketch on her desk. Frustrated, she looks toward ZYN's bowl. ZYN floats there, watching her.)

MIA

I guess you're my responsibility now.

ZYN

Thank you very much for accepting the role of caretaker. I think you'll find the position a source of endless joy and very few tangible benefits.

MIA

Of course. Now the fish is talking to me. I've been stalked by a cat and hunted a llama. Why not converse with a fish?

ZYN

I have been talking for some time. Petunia thinks I talk too much. We're fighting right now. Usually the male person speaks to me—

MIA

The male person? You mean G? Wait—who's Petunia?

ZYN

(of course)

The plant.

MIA

The plant talks too?

ZYN

Did you ever stop to think that everything has been talking all along and you simply weren't listening?

MIA

(numb)

I've been a little busy. I'm having a crisis.

(She holds up one of the proofs.)

MIA

Does this look like me?

ZYN

I cannot see the picture. The water is cloudy. Ammonia can reach toxic levels if it isn't managed properly. Perhaps the filter is jammed.

MIA

They took everything I gave them. My colors. My lines. All the little things I do. So why doesn't it feel like mine?

ZYN

Who knew a little thing like me could cause such a mess? It's embarrassing. The male person has a net. He puts me in a salad bowl while he cleans. It's a pleasant little vacation, and then I come home to beautiful, clean water.
