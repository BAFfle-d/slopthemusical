Scene 14 of 15 from the full one-act. Stage directions in the margins, lyrics in the light.

(MIA enters carrying a tablet, a small case, and a covered canvas. RON, KAREN, and CHAD sit at the table. LE CHAT watches from one side. JIM and BART linger near the machinery.)

RON

Mia—

MIA

I'm not walking out. I want to talk about what we do next.

(She sets down the canvas and opens the tablet.)

MIA

You built an incredible machine. Nobody's mad about the machine.

RON

So we're good?

MIA

We're good on the volume tier. Postcards, magnets, whatever's got a hook—I design that line myself, out of what the agent generates. It ships fast, it's cheap, and it's honest about what it is. You already have buyers.

RON

And that clears what we sank into the build?

MIA

Six weeks, by my math. Check it yourself.

(RON checks. The number works.)

CHAD

What's unit cost once we're actually fulfilling? Print, packaging, shipping—that eats margin fast on a two-dollar magnet.

MIA

Cheaper than anything you're running now. No revision cycle. One pass, straight to print. You'll have the cost sheet Friday.

(CHAD makes a note.)

KAREN

And the real commissions sitting next to that line—that's not muddy for the brand?

(MIA removes two small presentation boards from the case.)

MIA

Already named it.

(She reveals the first.)

MIA

Everyday MIAmi. The volume line. Fast, fun, and honest about what it is.

(She reveals the second.)

MIA

MIAmi Luxe. The premium tier. Same city. Different price point.

RON

I like that. That's a brand.

MIA

It's a boundary. You're welcome to like it too.

LE CHAT

And the originals?

MIA

Start with me. An actual painting, direct consultation, limited production. The agent handles application after I approve the source. When the numbers support it, we bring in apprentices.

CHAD

How many premium clients before the first apprentice?

MIA

Two.

LE CHAT

I know two boutiques on South Beach that want something defining, different, and impossible to mistake for anyone else's. Can you provide that?

MIA

Do you want to see?

(MIA places her hands on the covered canvas. MIA turns the painting. The blue-haired, spiky, ugly, majestic llama fills the canvas. Behind the company, the silhouette of the LLAMA rises. RON, KAREN, and CHAD remain fixed on the painting, the projections, and the potential product line. They never see the living silhouette behind them.)

LLAMA AND OPERATIC ENSEMBLE
Llama triumphans!
Ecce llama!
Ecce opus!
Manus facit!
Mens eligit!
Instrumentum—

RON

Stop. Stop, stop, stop.
